Add the error codes `body_too_large` (413) and `body_busy` (503). Core reads at most the cap of each route of a request body, and refuses a longer body before any parse. The cap is 64 KiB, 256 KiB for the Apple callback, and 8 MiB for the two SSO connection writes. A JSON body nested deeper than 16 or with more than 4096 items answers `invalid_body` before the parse. A form body of more than 128 pairs gets the refusal of a bad form before the parse, at the SAML ACS too. A SCIM body nested deeper than 16 or with more than 65536 items answers 400 `invalidSyntax` before the parse. The body reads of the routes with a cap above 64 KiB hold at most 16 MiB at once in one isolate. A read past it answers `body_busy` (503) with `Retry-After`. The compatibility package reads each request body with core's capped reader, at the cap of the core route of the path. Past that cap it answers `PAYLOAD_TOO_LARGE` (413) at its first read. Past the budget of the large reads it answers `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` (503). Before its read of an SSO connection write, it runs core's limit of the SSO manager writes of the user. Core counts that write once. Add the error code `body_timeout` (408). A body read above the floor stops when it gets no bytes for 10 s. It also stops when it runs past the time of its cap at 128 KiB per second, at least 10 s. Both answer `body_timeout`. One client holds at most 4 MiB of the budget of the large reads, or the cap of its read when that is more. The compatibility package answers `REQUEST_TIMEOUT` (408).
