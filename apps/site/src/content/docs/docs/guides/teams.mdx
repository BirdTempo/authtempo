**For:** an app developer whose organizations need named groups of members, such as "Design" or "Support".

**What it does:** core keeps teams inside each organization. A team groups members and has no role of its own.

## Before you start

- A working setup of [organizations](/docs/guides/organizations/).
- The roles of your organizations. [Roles and permissions](/docs/guides/roles-and-permissions/) gives the team actions.

## Steps

1. Add `organizations.teams` to the config.
2. Set `defaultTeam` to give each new organization one team with its creator.
3. Set `keepLastTeam` when an organization must always hold a team.
4. Set the limits `teamsPerOrganization` and `membersPerTeam` for the counts that your plan sells.

5. Call `client.organizations.teams.create({ organizationId, name })`.
6. Call `client.organizations.teams.members.add({ teamId, userId })` for a member of the organization.
7. Call `client.organizations.teams.setActive({ teamId })` when the person picks a team.

## How it behaves

### No team role

A team has no role. Every right comes from the organization role. A team member reads and writes only what the member's organization role allows.

| Write | The action it needs |
| --- | --- |
| Create, update, delete a team | `team:create`, `team:update`, `team:delete` |
| Add a person to a team | `member:update` |
| Remove another person from a team | `member:delete` |
| List the members of a team | `member:read` |
| Invite with `teamIds` | `member:update`, beside the invite rule |

- To add or remove another person, the caller also ranks at least as high as that person and holds each of their actions.
- A member may leave a team with their own `userId`.
- The five team writes need a verified address. The reads and `setActive` do not.
- A team name follows the organization name rule. Two teams may have the same name.

### The cascades

- A member removal and a leave delete the person's team rows in the same write. The person's sessions lose their active team.
- A team delete deletes its team rows. Each session in the team loses its active team, and each pending invitation loses the team.
- An organization delete deletes its teams.
- An accept adds the person to each team of the invitation's `teamIds` that still exists.
- A change of the active organization sets the active team to `null`.

### The limits

- `teamsPerOrganization`: a team create at the limit answers `team_limit`.
- `membersPerTeam`: a team member add at the limit answers `team_member_limit`. An accept to a full team answers the same, and the invitation stays pending.
- `keepLastTeam`: the delete of the last team answers `last_team`.
- Each count runs in the write's transaction, after a lock of the parent row. On Postgres, two writes at one moment cannot both pass a count.
- D1 transactions are not isolated. On D1, two writes at one moment can both pass a count.

### The hooks

The ten team [hook points](/docs/guides/hooks/) are `beforeCreateTeam`, `afterCreateTeam`, `beforeUpdateTeam`, `afterUpdateTeam`, `beforeDeleteTeam`, `afterDeleteTeam`, `beforeAddTeamMember`, `afterAddTeamMember`, `beforeRemoveTeamMember`, and `afterRemoveTeamMember`. A cascade fires no team hook. The default team and the team rows of an accept fire none either.

### The active team

`POST /organizations/teams/active` sets `session.activeTeamId`. The team must belong to the active organization, and the person must be in it. The value is a choice, not a right. Check the organization role on each request.

## If it fails

- [`forbidden_rank`](/docs/reference/errors/#forbidden_rank): the caller lacks the team or member action, or ranks below the person.
- [`email_unverified`](/docs/reference/errors/#email_unverified): a team write needs a verified address.
- [`team_limit`](/docs/reference/errors/#team_limit): the organization holds `teamsPerOrganization` teams.
- [`team_member_limit`](/docs/reference/errors/#team_member_limit): the team holds `membersPerTeam` members.
- [`last_team`](/docs/reference/errors/#last_team): `keepLastTeam` keeps the last team. Create another team first.
- [`already_member`](/docs/reference/errors/#already_member): the person is already in the team.
- [`conflict`](/docs/reference/errors/#conflict): another write changed the person at the same moment. Retry.
- [`hook_refused`](/docs/reference/errors/#hook_refused) and [`hook_failed`](/docs/reference/errors/#hook_failed): a before hook refused the write or threw.
- [`invalid_body`](/docs/reference/errors/#invalid_body): a bad name, a missing field, or another key.
- [`unauthorized`](/docs/reference/errors/#unauthorized): the route needs a session. A token answers 401.
